## 2. Private Fact Registry: Disallowed & Corrected Claims

The following claims were audited and permanently removed from public website copy:
1. **Removed**: Specific mechanical tolerances (e.g. "±0.2 mm calibration", "quad-press system", "4-stage sanding").
2. **Removed**: Absolute physical perfection assertions (e.g. "100% flatness", "zero core voids", "zero gaps").
3. **Removed**: Unsupported certifications (e.g. "IS:710 certified", "IS:303", "E0 / E1 compliance", "CARB P2", "FSC", "PEFC", "LEED credits"). A product variant named *Marine Plywood* does not author an unverified test report.
4. **Removed**: Specific test claims (e.g. "72-hour boiling water test", "specific density >850 kg/m³", "phenolic resin unextended"). Replaced with: *"Contact NEOM Panels for current product specifications and technical data."*
5. **Removed**: Unconditional claims (e.g. "100% waterproof", "fireproof", "termite-proof", "lifetime warranty").
6. **Removed**: Operational reach inflation (e.g. "active pan-India delivery network", "operating across all 6 GCC states", "owned high-tech automated factories"). Replaced with truthful staged roadmap language.
7. **Removed**: Fabricated software interfaces (e.g. "LIVE B2B PORTAL", "real-time GPS shipment tracking", "QR certificate scanner", "instant CAD/BIM downloads"). Replaced with truthful "Digital Platform Roadmap (Planned Systems)".
8. **Removed**: Sub-brand architecture from primary navigation (e.g. "Starwoods", "NEOM Packing Plywood", "NEOM Architectural"). NEOM Panels is the sole master brand.
9. **Removed**: Fabricated customer testimonials, client logos, awards, or fake project case studies.
