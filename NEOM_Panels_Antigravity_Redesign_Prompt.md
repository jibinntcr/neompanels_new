# NEOM Panels — Website Review and Antigravity Redesign Prompt

Prepared: 14 November 2026
Existing website: https://neompanels-new.vercel.app
Primary source: NEOM PANELS PitchDeck.pdf, 12 physical pages.

## Review findings

The existing site already uses NEOM's green palette, architectural imagery, the brand promise, product families and India/GCC growth narrative. It needs a clearer customer journey and tighter fidelity to the source deck, rather than simply more content.

| Observed issue | Required change |
| --- | --- |
| The homepage carries extensive market analysis, industry gaps, ecosystem pillars, GCC details, digital tools and revenue milestones. | Put products and enquiry first; move corporate strategy to About and Growth Vision. |
| At the inspected desktop viewport, the navigation was crowded, the sample button wrapped across three lines, and the right CTA was partly outside the visible viewport. | Reduce navigation items and validate every intermediate screen width. |
| The long hero headline dominates while the architectural image sits lower and smaller. | Create a more balanced opening with a larger architectural scene and a shorter supporting paragraph. |
| Hero and product content asserts ±0.2 mm tolerance, quad-press calibration, zero voids, IS:710/IS:303/E0, and 72-hour boiling-water performance. | Remove these claims unless approved evidence exists beyond the deck. The deck alone does not establish them. |
| The homepage says Pan-India + 6 GCC States and direct factory supply; About says the business has its own factory. | Reconcile these with the deck's staged manufacturing model and GCC expansion ambition. Do not infer present operational reach from a roadmap. |
| The site labels a dashboard LIVE B2B PORTAL and promises tracking, CAD/BIM, QR certificates, 48-hour sample ordering and other detailed capabilities. | Audit actual implementation. Present unimplemented capabilities as planned, and remove unsupported service promises. |
| The footer and About introduce Starwoods, NEOM Architectural and NEOM Packing Plywood brand architecture. | The supplied deck does not establish these brand relationships. Remove from the deck-only public narrative and record existing routes for owner review. |
| Homepage product-family cards contain nested category and product tabs. | Keep family discovery visible and move detail to dedicated product pages. |
| Strategic Blueprint 2026–2036 appears throughout utility navigation, hero and footer. | Keep the growth period in the corporate vision area; give customer tasks more prominence. |

Review scope: live homepage content and opening desktop design, plus About-page content. This was not a code audit, performance benchmark or full functional test of all routes.

Audit the following existing claims and remove them from public copy unless independently approved project documentation supports them:

- 100% flatness, zero core voids or zero core gaps.
- IS:710, IS:303, E0/E1, CARB P2, FSC, PEFC, LEED and fire ratings stated as NEOM certifications or compliance.
- 72-hour boiling-water performance, specific densities, resin chemistry, thicknesses, sample sizes and surface-hardness ratings.
- Unconditional waterproof, fireproof, termite-proof or structural/load-bearing claims.
- Pan-India distribution, active supply across all six GCC countries, owned automated factories and ready container-export capabilities.
- Live stock, live portals, shipment tracking, QR quality certificates, CAD/BIM libraries, instant quotes and guaranteed sample-delivery times.
- Customer logos, completed project claims, testimonials, awards, installed capacities and present revenue figures absent from the deck.

A product family named Marine Plywood does not establish a certification number or test result. Naming BWR/BWP variants does not authorise invented performance specifications. Do not transfer competitors' product properties to NEOM.

The four-stage business model and the India-first/GCC expansion plan must not be described as fully achieved. The deck does not state which operating stage has been completed.

Use NEOM Panels as the public master brand. The deck does not establish Starwoods, NEOM Architectural or NEOM Packing Plywood as approved sub-brands. Remove unsupported brand architecture from navigation and rewritten content. Inventory existing brand routes; redirect only to a genuinely relevant supported destination, otherwise preserve a truthful non-promotional legacy route or document its disposition. ### 16. Responsive behaviour, motion and accessibility

Support 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px widths. Test intermediate widths too. No horizontal overflow, cropped header buttons or navigation labels split across three lines. Use a clear mobile menu with keyboard operation, Escape handling, focus return and adequate touch targets.

Stack product families and content in a meaningful reading order. Use a vertical timeline on mobile. Do not force wide strategic diagrams onto small screens. Maintain visible focus, semantic headings, one H1 per page, labelled fields, descriptive link names, meaningful alt text and sufficient text/control contrast.

Use subtle 150–300 ms transitions and optional short scroll reveals that never hide content when JavaScript fails. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid autoplay carousels, scroll hijacking, heavy parallax, mouse cursors and unnecessary WebGL.

### 17. Engineering, search and performance

Retain the existing stack and avoid unnecessary dependencies or a framework migration. Use reusable layout, product-card, enquiry, application and CTA components. Keep source-supported content in structured data where appropriate. Do not remove working functionality without reviewing it.

Set canonical URLs only after verifying the production domain configuration. Add organisation structured data only from supported identity/contact information. Avoid invented reviews, ratings, offers, addresses and certifications in metadata as well as visible copy.

Optimise responsive images, reserve dimensions, prioritise the hero asset, lazy-load below-fold images and minimise animation/client bundles. Check Core Web Vitals where measurable; aim for LCP ≤2.5 s, CLS ≤0.1 and INP ≤200 ms under the stated test conditions. Report actual measurements and method, not guaranteed scores.
